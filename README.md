## 🎨 Project Overview

**Interior Flame By Dia** is a creative brand website for showcasing handcrafted artwork, wearable art, personalized gifts, and distinctive pieces for everyday living.

The experience is centered on the work of **Habiba Dia**. It brings product discovery, artist storytelling, and custom-order enquiries together in a single brand destination. Rather than presenting artwork as a simple catalogue, the website gives visitors context about the creator and a clear route from inspiration to a potential commission.

**Live project:** https://interior-flame-by-dia.pages.dev/

## ✨ The Brand

Interior Flame By Dia is built around the idea that art can be part of everyday life: something to wear, gift, display, or commission for a particular person or occasion.

The site supports that identity through three connected themes:

- **Original expression** — handmade work with a distinct creative point of view.
- **Personal meaning** — custom pieces shaped around a customer's idea or occasion.
- **Art beyond the canvas** — creative work across fashion, accessories, gifts, and home décor.

The goal of the digital experience is to make the brand approachable while preserving the individuality of the work.

## 🛍️ Customer Journey

The experience is organized around two main paths.

### Path A — Explore the collection

1. **Arrive** — understand the brand and its creative direction.
2. **Discover** — browse the categories and featured work.
3. **Explore** — continue to the shop or relevant product destination.
4. **Enquire** — use the contact route provided by the website when more information is needed.

### Path B — Request a custom piece

1. **Share the idea** — communicate the concept, occasion, or inspiration.
2. **Discuss the details** — align on the style and requirements.
3. **Create the artwork** — produce the piece according to the agreed brief.
4. **Arrange delivery** — confirm the final handover with the brand.

The steps above describe the custom-order journey communicated by the site; they should not be interpreted as an automated order-management workflow.

## 🖌️ Design Philosophy

The interface should serve the artwork, not compete with it.

- **Artwork first:** imagery and product presentation should remain the visual focus.
- **Clear hierarchy:** headings, supporting text, and calls to action should be easy to scan.
- **Editorial storytelling:** brand narrative adds meaning to product discovery.
- **Intentional simplicity:** avoid unnecessary interface elements that distract from the work.
- **Consistent identity:** maintain coherent typography, spacing, colour, and image treatment across sections.
- **Subtle motion:** use animation sparingly and only when it helps orientation or feedback.
- **Mobile-conscious layouts:** keep navigation, product browsing, and contact actions usable on smaller screens.

## ♿ Accessibility and Performance

These are recommended quality targets and should be validated through testing rather than treated as completed audits.

### Accessibility
- Use semantic HTML landmarks and a logical heading order.
- Provide descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.
- Ensure links and controls are keyboard accessible.
- Maintain visible focus indicators.
- Check text contrast and readable type sizes.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences where animations are used.

### Performance
- Serve appropriately sized, compressed images.
- Avoid loading large assets before they are needed.
- Use responsive images and lazy loading where appropriate.
- Keep JavaScript and animation overhead proportionate to the experience.
- Test Core Web Vitals with [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/).
